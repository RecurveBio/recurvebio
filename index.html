Our focus

Building value into the wastewater treatment journey

Industrial wastewater is abundant and costly to treat. RecurveBio is developing a modular biological conversion approach that uses these streams as feedstocks, starting with brewery and food-manufacturing applications.

The RecurveBio platform

Adaptable biology. Scalable process. Multiple end markets.

The platform brings together feedstock assessment, microalgae cultivation and downstream product development.

  • Industrial feedstocks: nutrient-rich wastewater across sites, batches and seasons.
  • Proprietary biology: culture matching and process optimisation for variable conditions.
  • Scalable process: repeatable cultivation and bioprocessing designed for industrial operation.
  • High-value products: biomass, proteins, lipids and functional bio-based ingredients.
