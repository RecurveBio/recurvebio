Our Mission

RecurveBio exists to advance biological innovation through scientific curiosity, rigorous research, and collaborative thinking. Our ambition is to contribute to technologies and solutions that improve human health and deepen our understanding of biological systems.

About

Recurve Bio is developing an algal biotechnology platform that converts industrial wastewater into sustainable oils and other high-value bioproducts. Our initial focus is on brewery and distillery effluent streams, helping manufacturers reduce waste treatment costs while creating new sources of value. By combining wastewater remediation with resource recovery, we aim to support the transition to a circular bioeconomy. We are currently advancing proof-of-concept activities and building strategic partnerships across industry and academia, with a long-term vision of producing sustainable alternatives to conventional agricultural oils from industrial waste streams.
